# Play Maya Robotics

1. Open **Play-MayaRobotics.cmd**, wait for the black Maya loading screen, and choose **Start Shift**. You begin with **$0 and 100% battery**. Reach **$100 or more in net cash** to win the shift.
2. While safely stopped with empty cargo, press **N** or **Choose** to browse delivery offers. Select **Across the block** for the longer route with turns and two signal crossings. Accept the displayed offer with **J** or its button. Use **W/S** to drive/reverse, **A/D** to turn, and **Space** to brake. While reversing, **S+A** backs the rear left and **S+D** backs it right; **A/D** without W/S still turns the drone. Hold **Left Shift** while driving to use **Boost**: extra speed on the road and extra motor power when climbing a curb. Boost uses more battery, including while the motors push against a curb. Release Left Shift to return to normal power.
3. Stop in the **green pickup ring**. Press **E** or remain safely stopped inside for **ten continuous seconds**. The doors open; one or two boxes gradually appear two feet above the vehicle and lower into the front compartment or both compartments. The doors close after the parcels are secured.
4. Follow the route and radar to the **red delivery ring**. Stop and press **E**, or stay for ten seconds. The doors open, the boxes gently rise and fade away, and the doors close before delivery completes.
5. A successful **one-parcel job pays $10**; a successful **two-parcel job pays $16 total**. An ordinary failed job pays **$0**. Unless the drone is totalled, choose the next delivery with **N** or replay with **Enter**. Replay does not reset your cash or refill your battery.

## Battery, time and cargo

- **Recharge:** green rings and lightning-bolt map markers identify **Maya Depot** and **Community Clinic**. With the doors closed, stop safely in a charging ring for **ten continuous seconds**. Charging then adds **5 battery percentage points per second**, while you remain parked. It costs **$2 once per visit**, charged when the battery first gains energy. A full battery costs nothing; you need at least $2 to begin a recharge. Drive away when ready. There is no charging key, post or cable.
- **Plan the route:** two secured parcels have **30% shorter battery endurance** than one under the same activity. Boost gives extra speed and more wheel power, but drains battery faster even when pushing against a curb. Steeper curbs may require a different approach. The promised delivery timer continues through driving, cargo service and charging. **P** or **Escape** opens Pause; menus pause the delivery and charging clocks.
- **Traffic and cargo:** you may bump a stationary car or a car driving away; normal cargo damage still applies. A moving car hitting Maya head-on or rear-ending it **totals the drone and ends the whole shift**. Choose **Start Over** and confirm to begin again. Small qualifying bumps cost **0.5 percentage points** of condition; harder impacts and qualifying curb drops cost **1 point per impact episode**. Deliver within the promise and with **at least 80% condition** to succeed. Condition applies to the whole delivery, including a two-parcel job.
- **Ring visits:** leaving resets an unfinished ten-second countdown. If Maya is moving or unsafe, stop and let it settle. Pickup/delivery service takes priority at the active destination; the same location can become a green charging ring after service. Leaving and entering again starts a new charging visit, which may cost another $2 if energy is needed.
- **Recovery and starting over:** during an active delivery or after an ordinary job failure, **R** can recover Maya to the depot when safe, preserving cash and battery. After completing a delivery at zero battery, press **N**, then **R** to recover; recharge still requires payment. Battery continues to drain while driving between jobs. **R and per-job replay cannot recover a totalled drone.** Its **Start Over** confirmation starts a new shift with **$0 cash, zero completed jobs, 100% battery, 100% condition and empty cargo**; the old job and shift progress are cleared. At zero battery with less than $2, **Start New Shift** also starts fresh. After winning, **Play Again** starts a fresh shift. Display, audio and accessibility settings remain saved.

## Menus, camera and display

Use the mouse, **Tab/arrow keys**, and **Enter** to navigate menus. **Escape** returns to the previous menu; **P** resumes from Pause. A held movement key must be released before it can drive Maya after leaving a menu.

Press **M** during gameplay to open the **whole-city map**, or choose **Pause > City Map**. The map pauses the game, including delivery and charging timers. Choose **Zoom to Delivery** to focus on the delivery route, Maya and the actual charging locations; **Show Whole City** returns to the city overview. Use the mouse or **Tab/arrow keys and Enter** for map actions. **M**, **Escape** or **Back** closes the map and returns to the screen that opened it; a map opened from Pause returns to Pause.

Press **C** during gameplay to switch between the **chase camera** and Maya's **front POV**, then press C again to return. Chase suppresses chassis shake. POV adds a small amount of measured road motion: **3% before smoothing and amplitude limits**, with a steady horizon. In chase view, hold the **right mouse button** and drag to orbit; **Home** recentres the camera. The saved chase orbit returns after leaving POV, while the robot can still react visibly to road motion.

**Settings** controls resolution, window mode, V-Sync, master volume, UI scale, text size and high contrast. Choose **Apply Changes** to save. Display changes have a **15-second Keep/Revert** confirmation and revert automatically if not kept. Back discards unapplied changes. Your chosen settings persist across launches; the first run without saved settings defaults to a 1280 x 720 window.

The Atlas Noir HUD uses charcoal panels, warm white text and amber accents. It keeps cash, battery, speed, parcel condition and remaining delivery time at the edges. Pickup and charging markers stay green; delivery markers stay red. Driving infobox backgrounds and their sharp borders are partly transparent, while text and icons stay opaque and readable. Only the circular minimap and its rim stay solid. Brief control hints stay visible for 5.5 seconds, fade over 1.5 seconds, and return on a new stage or after pausing. Open Pause > Controls for the complete key reference. HUD panels avoid covering the drone. Hollow amber direction arrows pulse gently and guide you toward the next turn. The city-block mission uses a local map that follows Maya; after delivery, the route can guide you back to the depot. Crossing cues report the available city signal state. Yield to traffic and check the road before crossing; the game does not brake or steer for you. If signal information is unavailable, the display says so.

**Join the Project** opens **https://quantellics.com/maya** in your operating system's default browser. The game stays on its menu. Use **Quit Game** and confirm to exit. Shift progress is not saved.

Keep the complete release folder together, including the **DDSCity** and **Engine** folders. This is a local Windows build.
