# Store copy draft

**Title:** Maya - Delivery Drone Simulator

## Short description

Drive Maya, a six-wheel ground delivery drone, through a city. Pick up parcels, protect your cargo, manage battery power and beat delivery deadlines. Complete jobs and reach $100 in net cash to finish your shift.

## About this game

Take the wheel of Maya, a compact six-wheel delivery robot working a city shift. Choose a job, navigate to the pickup, carry one or two parcels and deliver them before the promised time runs out. Every trip is a balance between speed, battery power and cargo condition.

### Earn your shift

Start with $0 and a full battery. Successful one-parcel jobs pay $10; two-parcel jobs pay $16. Reach at least $100 in net cash to complete the shift. Charging costs $2 per qualifying visit, so a fast route is only part of a good delivery.

### Drive with care

Use six-wheel ground movement, braking and Boost to negotiate roads and curbs. Boost adds motor power and speed while using more battery. Impacts can damage cargo, and a moving car striking the robot head-on or from behind can end the shift. Arrive within the deadline with at least 80% parcel condition.

### Plan the next stop

Follow the direction arrows, minimap and whole-city map. Park in the pickup or delivery ring and begin service with a key press or by remaining safely stopped for ten seconds. Watch the doors and cargo move through loading and unloading. Find a charging location before your battery runs out.

### Current features

- Four selectable delivery offers, including a longer city-block route with turns and signal crossings.
- One- and two-parcel jobs, delivery deadlines, cargo condition and a $100 shift goal.
- Battery management, paid charging and Boost for additional road speed and curb power.
- Chase and front POV cameras, route guidance, minimap and a pausing whole-city map.
- Resolution and window settings, volume, UI scale, text size and high contrast options.

### Current scope

This version is a single-player Windows game controlled with keyboard and mouse. Maya is a wheeled ground robot; there is no aerial flight. Shift progress is not saved between sessions. Traffic signals provide guidance; players must brake and steer themselves.
