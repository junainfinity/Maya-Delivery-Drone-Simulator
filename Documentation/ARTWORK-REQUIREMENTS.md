# Steam artwork and capture requirements

Checked 10 October 2026 (Asia/Kolkata). Dimensions below describe required upload targets, not certification that a particular file passed upload or design review.

## Store assets

| Asset | Upload dimensions | Content |
|---|---:|---|
| Header capsule | 920 × 430 | Artwork and readable game title |
| Small capsule | 462 × 174 | Readable title at reduced display sizes |
| Main capsule | 1232 × 706 | Artwork and readable game title |
| Vertical capsule | 748 × 896 | Artwork and readable game title |
| Screenshots | At least five; minimum 1920 × 1080, 16:9 | Actual gameplay captures |
| Page background (optional) | 1438 × 810 | Subtle background artwork |

Do not place concept art, AI artwork, pre-rendered stills, slogans, awards or product descriptions into the screenshot upload slots. Review the all-ages flag for actual captures; mark at least four suitable images when their content qualifies. Source: [Store Graphical Assets](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/assets/standard).

## Library assets

| Asset | Upload dimensions | Content |
|---|---:|---|
| Library capsule | 600 × 900 | Artwork and game title |
| Library header | 920 × 430 | Artwork and game title |
| Library hero | 3840 × 1240 PNG | Artwork only, **no words** |
| Library logo | Either 1280 px wide and/or 720 px tall, transparent PNG | Game logotype, optional logomark |

Use the official hero template to check cropping: the documented safe area is 860 × 380. Check logo placement in the Steamworks preview. Source: [Library Assets](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/assets/libraryassets).

## Icons

| Asset | Upload dimensions / format | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Shortcut icon | 256 × 256 or 512 × 512 PNG / ICO | Steam can generate the ICO from PNG |
| App icon | 184 × 184 JPG | Opaque background; check small-size recognition |
| Editable master (project convenience) | High-resolution PNG | Not a separate required Steam upload |

An icon file in this kit does not automatically replace the executable's embedded icon or an existing desktop shortcut. Source: [Community and Client Icons](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/assets/community).

## Design acceptance

1. Use only game artwork, the game name and official subtitle on permanent capsules; exclude scores, discount copy, awards, calls to action and unrelated promotions.
2. Make the logo readable at Steam's actual display size, including the small capsule's 120 × 45 rendering.
3. Keep library hero text-free and library logo transparent. Use artwork appropriate for general store discovery.
4. Show a wheeled robot consistent with the game. Avoid flying rotors, weapons or features absent from the current build.
5. Record AI-assisted marketing provenance in the [survey draft](CONTENT-SURVEY-AI-DRAFT.md); keep genuine gameplay captures separately labeled.

Source: [Graphical Asset Rules](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/assets/rules).

## Five gameplay capture targets

1. Chase view driving the robot with readable route and battery HUD.
2. Pickup with the real open compartment and loading parcel.
3. Two-parcel transport through the city-block route.
4. Delivery/unloading at the actual destination.
5. Charging with actual battery/cash feedback visible.

Capture these from the final gameplay build. A screenshot selection from earlier QA must be reviewed for version accuracy before Steam submission; marketing artwork is never a substitute.
