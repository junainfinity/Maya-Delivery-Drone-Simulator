# Gameplay trailer capture plan

**Status: proposed; no completed trailer is asserted by this document.**

Produce a roughly 60-second gameplay-first trailer from the final packaged game. The suggested edit length is a project choice, not a Steam duration rule. Show the controls and actual player experience with the HUD visible; any accelerated sequence must be clearly understandable and must not imply a feature absent from the build.

| Time | Real gameplay shot | Purpose |
|---:|---|---|
| 0–8 seconds | Chase driving toward an actual pickup with Maya clear in frame | Establish the wheeled robot and delivery loop immediately |
| 8–20 seconds | Safe stop; open doors; one or two parcels load | Show what the player collects |
| 20–35 seconds | City-block route, turn guidance, POV/chase and a measured Boost segment | Show driving and navigation |
| 35–48 seconds | Charging, delivery deadline/condition, actual unloading and payment | Show resource tradeoffs and rewards |
| 48–60 seconds | Successful shift progress, final driving shot, brief game-title end card | Finish with the current game identity |

Do not use AI key art as gameplay footage. Do not substitute a Blender asset animation for native cargo/driving footage. Avoid console windows, diagnostics, personal information, developer paths and unrelated overlays. Use only licensed music/audio and preserve its credit information.

Steam requires a trailer; the official guide recommends primarily gameplay for the first trailer. Target H.264 video with AAC stereo audio in MP4, 1920 × 1080, 30 or 60 fps and at least 5,000 Kbps. Steam also accepts documented alternative formats. Review the transcoded upload, its selected category, thumbnail and poster before submission. Source: [Trailers](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/trailer).
