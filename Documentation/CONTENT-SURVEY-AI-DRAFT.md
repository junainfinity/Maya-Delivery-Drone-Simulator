# Content survey and AI disclosure draft

**Not a submitted survey or an assigned age rating.** Prepared 10 October 2026 (Asia/Kolkata); the publisher must verify against every file uploaded to Steam, including inaccessible content.

## Known game content

The current design is a city delivery simulation with a six-wheel robot, one or two parcels, traffic encounters, vehicle damage, battery management and fictional delivery earnings. A moving car can total the robot and end the shift. These details should be disclosed accurately when the survey asks about violence or vehicle impacts; do not assume a rating from the non-combat theme.

The City Sample environment can contain background characters, props, signs, audio and unused sample content. Sexual content, nudity, profanity, gambling, substances and other survey categories require an inventory review; no blanket “none” declaration is made here. Review uncooked source dependencies separately from the exact cooked depot. Confirm all generated survey results and required regional ratings in Steamworks.

## Draft AI statement

> AI tools assisted the creation of promotional key artwork and game icons used in the publishing kit and repository presentation. These promotional illustrations are separate from gameplay screenshots. They are reviewed against the game's wheeled delivery robot and current features. Authentic screenshots and trailer footage are captured from the actual game.

Before submission, replace this draft with an inventory of the actual assets used on the store page and in the delivered build. If generated artwork is embedded into menus, loading screens, icons or other player-visible build content, include that placement. Review existing game artwork, sound, narrative and localization as well as this newly generated marketing kit.

## Survey handling

1. Record which shipped, player-consumed assets were generated or assisted by AI and identify them as pre-generated where applicable.
2. Distinguish AI-assisted coding/tool productivity from generated art/audio/text consumed by players; do not use the former as a reason to omit the latter.
3. Verify whether any runtime generative service exists. This preparation task creates no such service; existing integration remains to be audited. If present, disclose it and its illegal-content safeguards.
4. Confirm ownership/licensing and consistency between the build and its marketing. Generated artwork does not bypass rights obligations.
5. Ask Steamworks Support about classification if store-only AI artwork does not fit the current survey wording; the official page primarily defines content that ships with the game and is consumed by players.

Official source: [Content Survey](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/contentsurvey). The current survey covers general content, mature content and generative AI; accurate inventory is required before review.
