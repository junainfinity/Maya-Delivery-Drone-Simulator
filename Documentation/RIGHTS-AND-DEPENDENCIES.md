# Rights and dependency inventory

This kit does not grant ownership of third-party assets or complete commercial release clearance. Publisher verification is required for the exact source/assets/binaries distributed.

## Public repository scope

Publish project-owned C++ source, project configuration, original documentation and the prepared marketing artwork only where ownership permits. Preserve existing notices and do not apply a blanket open-source license to Epic or marketplace materials. A public repository does not automatically mean all files are open source; use the repository's actual license or rights notice.

Do not commit City Sample content, its copied sample C++ modules, engine source/binaries, marketplace robot assets, Megascans source assets, extracted third-party plugins, paid packages or private test evidence. Acquire these independently under their actual terms. Project-owned Unreal assets also remain separate if their provenance or derivative dependencies are unresolved.

## Source versus cooked distribution

Epic's Content License Agreement distinguishes distribution inside an inseparable object-code project from source-format licensed-content redistribution. It restricts standalone source sharing and adds service/content-specific terms; those terms must be checked for each acquired package. Consequently, this repository excludes publisher source packages even where a licensed cooked game may use them. This is a packaging decision, not a finding that every current asset is cleared.

Official source: [Epic Content License Agreement](https://www.unrealengine.com/eula/content). Check the applicable agreement accepted at acquisition, any [Unreal Engine license](https://www.unrealengine.com/eula/unreal), and publisher-specific terms before commercial distribution. Do not upload protected source packages to make the public checkout self-contained.

## Credit preparation

Prepare a final game credits/third-party notices file from the verified dependency inventory. Confirm the exact engine attribution language and trademark notices under the applicable engine agreement. Do not invent a publisher identity, licensing grant or ownership assertion in place of missing records.
