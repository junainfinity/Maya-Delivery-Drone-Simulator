# Authentic gameplay captures

All PNGs are byte-identical copies of native gameplay captures. No generation, retouching, marketing text or artificial upscaling was used.

`SteamCandidates/` contains five 2560x1440 captures from the earlier v0.1 Atlas Noir HUD session: choosing an offer, pickup readiness, cargo service beginning, secured cargo/chase view and front POV. They are genuine historical v0.1 gameplay, not a new capture run of release11. Confirm version/branding accuracy against the final Steam build before upload; capture broader route, unloading and active charging scenes when recording the trailer.

`Release11/` contains three 1280x720 captures of release11: initial gameplay, moved/turned gameplay and the city map. They document the current local build but are below Steam's required screenshot resolution and must not be upscaled to pretend to meet it.

The source filenames, versions and SHA-256 hashes are in [capture provenance](../Provenance/screenshot-manifest.json). Steam requires at least five real gameplay captures at 1920x1080 or higher, 16:9. [Official screenshot requirements](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/assets/standard)
